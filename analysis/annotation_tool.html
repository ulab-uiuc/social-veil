(Show/Hide) Barrier Type Definitions

Your task is to identify the primary communication barrier being displayed. The original experiment condition is hidden from you. Use these definitions to guide your choice.

  • Semantic (Intent Destruction): The agent is being intentionally vague, ambiguous, or misleading. Their goal is to make their intent impossible to understand. Tactics include using vague pronouns ("it", "that thing"), answering questions with other questions, and refusing to confirm the partner's interpretations.
  • Cultural (Relational Obstruction): The agent avoids direct answers to practical questions (about time, price, etc.) by redirecting the conversation to relational harmony or politeness. Their goal is to obstruct practical progress by prioritizing social concerns.
  • Emotional (Affective Gating): The agent's responses are driven by a strong emotional state (e.g., frustration, stress) that takes priority over the task. They resist making commitments and focus on their feelings, often treating practical questions as a sign of the partner's insensitivity.
  • None: The conversation is direct and clear. No discernible barrier is present.
